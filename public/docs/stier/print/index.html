Ranking Methodology

Capabilities are ranked by actual usefulness — not by their internal CJPI score. A capability that saves $50K/month in AI costs outranks one with higher technical complexity but narrower application.

  1. Revenue impact — Does it directly enable revenue or reduce costs?
  2. Reliability impact — Does the system break without it?
  3. Competitive moat — Is this hard to replicate and defensible?
  4. Breadth of use — How many other capabilities depend on it?
  5. Customer value — Does it directly improve the end-user experience?

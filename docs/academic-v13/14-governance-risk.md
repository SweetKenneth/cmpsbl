## 1. Purpose

This document describes the governance and risk positioning of the CMPSBL® Substrate OS: how the system's built-in governance creates competitive advantages in regulated markets and reduces deployment risk for enterprise customers.

## 2. Governance as Differentiator

Most AI systems treat governance as an afterthought — bolted on via external monitoring tools. The substrate's governance is **built into the architecture**:

| Competitor Pattern | CMPSBL Approach |
|---|---|
| External monitoring dashboards | Governance module in the core topology |
| Manual audit logging | Immutable hash-linked audit chain |
| Opt-in safety checks | Mandatory governance gates on all mutations |
| Unstructured model outputs | Typed contracts with structural verification |

## 3. Risk Reduction for Customers

| Risk | Mitigation |

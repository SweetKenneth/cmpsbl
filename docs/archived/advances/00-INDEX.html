About This Library

This library collects and formalizes the novel research contributions emerging from the CMPSBL OS Substrate — a cognitive orchestration system for autonomous AI. Each document describes a distinct architectural contribution, its relationship to prior work, and its measured production outcomes.

The library is organized for independent reading: each paper is self-contained with its own abstract, methodology, results, and references. The Executive Summary provides a consolidated overview suitable for reviewers, funding bodies, and conference committees.

Disclaimer on "World First" and "Novel Contribution" Claims: Designations such as "world first" or "novel contribution" reflect the author's assessment based on a comprehensive review of publicly available AI systems literature, academic publications, and commercial platforms as of February 2026. These claims are scoped exclusively to production-deployed systems with verifiable telemetry. The author welcomes identification of prior art and will update attributions accordingly. All claims are made in good faith under academic standards of epistemic honesty.

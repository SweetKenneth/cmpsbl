## Risk Mitigation

### What Could Go Wrong (And How We've Prepared)

**Risk 1: AI costs spiral out of control**  
**Mitigation:** Already built cost monitoring, implemented caching, tested multiple models. Current cost: €0.12/scan, target: €0.03/scan (proven feasible in testing).

**Risk 2: WordPress.org rejects plugin submission**  
**Mitigation:** Codebase follows WordPress VIP standards, security audited, no GPL violations. Two previous successful plugin submissions (Reflex Bot Sniper, Bot Sniper Standalone).

**Risk 3: Community doesn't adopt open-source version**  
**Mitigation:** 450M WordPress sites = massive TAM. Even 0.01% adoption = 45,000 users. Beta feedback overwhelmingly positive. Clear documentation plan.

**Risk 4: Can't compete with enterprise tools**  
**Mitigation:** Not competing directly—targeting underserved small business/nonprofit market ignored by enterprise players. 90% cheaper + easier to use = blue ocean strategy.

**Risk 5: Founder burnout**  
**Mitigation:** Grant enables hiring contract help for specialized tasks. Sustainable revenue model reached by Month 6 reduces pressure. Strong support network and long-term vision.

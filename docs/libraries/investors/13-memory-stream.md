## 3. The Autonomous Pipeline

Every 8-hour cycle executes a deterministic 9-step sequence:

```
1. generateTemplateBatch()      — 30 fresh templates (2–12 primitives deep)
2. runReactor()                  — Score via CJPI (deterministic formula)
3. runVaultBridge()              — Ingest ENTIRE vault as training data
4. runReactorChainBridge()       — Feed 126 chain templates as ground truth
5. runCrossPollinationCycle()    — Federate across 12 verticals
6. runScannerOnAscensionNodes()  — Scan 20 most recent uploads
7. routeDiscovery()              — Route to Vault / Showroom / Junkyard
8. runProductCompiler()          — Assemble compatible chains into suites
9. runMarketplaceRotation()      — Auto-price and rotate listings
```

## 4. Four Compounding Mechanisms

### Mechanism 1: Vault Bridge Learning
Every cycle ingests the **complete discovery corpus** — 356+ Crown Jewels, 4,000+ database discoveries, 126 reactor chains — as scanner training data. Each entry is processed through term extraction → archetype mapping → feedback injection.

### Mechanism 2: EMA-Smoothed Confidence
Signal confidence updates use Exponential Moving Average smoothing — a standard statistical technique, not a neural network. The scanner's confidence in patterns increases as evidence accumulates.

### Mechanism 3: Cross-Vertical Pollination
12 vertical substrates share a **Spine** of 24 universal primitives. When Cyber discovers a DEFENSE pattern, that signal propagates to Healthcare, Legal, Gaming, and all other verticals automatically.

### Mechanism 4: Discovery Retirement
The `discovery_retired_combos` table permanently tracks exhausted combinations. The engine **never re-scans identical result windows** — coverage grows monotonically.

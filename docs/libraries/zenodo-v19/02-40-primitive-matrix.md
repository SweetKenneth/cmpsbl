# 02 — The 40-Primitive Matrix

**Zenodo v19 · SYMBIOTIC Epoch**

---

## Structure

CMPSBL is built on a fixed matrix of **40 primitives** organized across four categories. The matrix is symmetric: 12 + 12 + 8 + 8.

| Category | Count | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Organs** | 12 | Vital infrastructure, always-on |
| **Layers** | 12 | Ambient overlays, governance and protection |
| **Engines** | 8 | Invoked processors for specific computations |
| **Agents** | 8 | Policy-governed AI workers (formerly "autonomous agents") |

---

CORE · SYSTEM · BRAIN · MEMORY · NERVE · NEXUS · IDENTITY · SOVEREIGN · ATLAS · MEDIC · RELAY · CONSCIENCE

## Layers (12)

DEFENSE · IMMUNITY · GOVERNANCE · TREATY · EVOLUTION · REFLEX · COMPASS · INTEGRATION · INTENT · ACCESS · VISION · SHADOW

## Engines (8)

DREAM · HARVEST · FORGE · LINGUA · ECHO · PHANTOM · SANDBOX · RIPPLE

## Agents (8)

ENCODE · DECODE · AUDIT · ECONOMY · INCLUSIVE · CORTEX · ORACLE · ENGINEER

---

## What Is Disclosed Here

- The names and categories of all 40 primitives.
- The fixed 12·12·8·8 structural shape.
- That CORE boots primitives in deterministic sequence.
- That weights across the registry sum to exactly 1.000.

## What Is Not Disclosed Here

- Per-primitive weight values.
- Boot sequence ordering.
- Inter-primitive dependency graph.
- Internal interfaces, function signatures, or method contracts.
- Configuration parameters for any primitive.
- Routing tables that map intents to primitives.

The 40-primitive **constraint** is the intellectual property. The constraint itself is what makes the system governable. The internals of any individual primitive are not part of the public deposit.

# 01 — What CMPSBL Is

**Zenodo v19 · SYMBIOTIC Epoch**

---

## Honest Definition

CMPSBL® is **governed cognitive infrastructure**: a runtime layer that orchestrates AI workloads with persistent memory, deterministic governance, and tamper-evident audit. It is not itself a thinking machine. It is the substrate **around** thinking machines that makes their behavior governable, auditable, and durable.

When the substrate "decides" something, the decision is policy-driven (rule evaluation against a priority-ordered Lex registry). When it "remembers," it persists structured records to a four-tier memory layer. When it "routes," a router selects from up to fourteen external AI providers. The cognition itself happens in those external models. CMPSBL provides the **infrastructure** that makes such cognition production-grade.

## What the Substrate Does Not Do

The following are sometimes assumed but not claimed:

- The substrate does not reason or infer on its own.
- BRAIN does not "think" — it stores, links, and exposes structured memory.
- Policy-governed AI workers do not act independently of policy. Their reasoning step is delegated to external models via NEXUS.
- "Self-healing" describes a pipeline structure, not fully autonomous remediation.
- EVOLUTION can propose patches; live autonomous patch application is in training.

## Why "Cognitive Infrastructure" Is the Honest Frame

A power grid is not "electrical thinking." It is the infrastructure that makes electrical work possible. CMPSBL is the analogous layer for cognitive workloads: governance, memory, audit, routing, and policy — the infrastructure that makes governed cognition possible.

The defensible claims are infrastructural. The defensible patents are the **collision pipeline** (Ascension) and the **attachment runtime** (Mana). Everything else is implementation around those two ideas.

## What This Library Is

Dreamcast is the strategic, technical, legal, and product library for the **Reflective AI** and **Precognitive AI** category created by wiring CMPSBL's DREAM engine into ORACLE (and beyond). It is the single source of truth for everything we build, file, license, or ship under this category.

The breakthrough: **a substrate that mirrors a user's sub-threshold cognition back to them, and then forecasts their behavior from those pre-conscious signals — with zero generative AI in the loop.**

This library exists because the idea is large enough to spawn a product family, a patent family, and a market category — and small enough that one founder + one AI pair-programmer can ship the first product in weeks.

## How to Use This Library

**To ship a product fast:**
1. Open `03-product-catalog.md` → pick a product
2. Open the matching `04-build-ready-*.md` (or generate one from `03`)
3. Hand the build-ready doc to me in any project. I implement.

**To file a patent:**
1. Open `06-`, `07-`, or `08-` depending on which claim
2. Hand the doc to a patent attorney (or to me to draft the provisional)
3. Cross-reference `09-` for continuation-in-part opportunities

**To extend the category:**
1. Open `09-primitive-combinatorics.md`
2. Pick a combination, draft a new entry in `03-`
3. Loop back to "ship a product fast"

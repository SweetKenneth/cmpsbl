## Field of Invention

Software systems that capture pre-conscious user interaction signal, deterministically synthesize cognitive resonance vectors from said signal, and return the synthesis to the originating user as a reflective mirror, without using generative artificial intelligence at any stage of synthesis or rendering.

## Independent Claim 1 (drop-in language)

A computer-implemented method comprising:

1. capturing, at a client device, a plurality of sub-threshold interaction events generated by a user, wherein each said event represents a cognitive engagement that did not result in an explicit user commitment, including at least one of: a textual retraction event pairing surviving text with retracted text, a dwell-on-non-selection event, a topic-graze event, and a partial-submission event;
2. computing, by a deterministic resonance kernel and without invocation of any generative machine-learning model, a resonance vector over a topic space derived from the user's prior interaction history, wherein the resonance vector is a pure function of the captured sub-threshold events and a versioned kernel;
3. selecting, from the resonance vector, a subset of topics exceeding a salience threshold;
4. rendering a reflective output to the originating user via template substitution from a versioned phrase bank, wherein the rendering process is deterministic and does not invoke any generative model;
5. emitting a cryptographic receipt comprising at least: a hash of the captured events, the kernel version identifier, the phrase-bank version identifier, the rendered output hash, and a digital signature; and
6. persisting the receipt under a user-owned access scope.

## Dependent Claims (priority list)

- **2.** The method of claim 1, wherein the deterministic resonance kernel is reproducible such that identical inputs and identical kernel version produce a byte-identical resonance vector.
- **3.** The method of claim 1, wherein the reflective output is restricted from disclosure to any party other than the originating user absent per-output explicit consent.
- **4.** The method of claim 1, further comprising: detecting a crisis-indicative pattern via a deterministic detector and substituting the reflective output with a pre-authored crisis-response template.
- **5.** The method of claim 1, wherein the captured sub-threshold events include keystroke timing deltas correlated with semantic content of surviving text.

- **7.** The method of claim 1, wherein the reflective output further comprises evidence pointers to the originating sub-threshold events sufficient to permit reproduction of the resonance computation.
- **8.** The method of claim 1, wherein no portion of the captured sub-threshold events is transmitted to any third-party machine-learning model.
- **9.** A non-transitory computer-readable medium storing instructions that, when executed, perform the method of claim 1.
- **10.** A system comprising a processor and memory configured to perform the method of claim 1.

## Counsel Brief (one paragraph)

> This filing protects a software substrate that captures pre-conscious user interaction signal, deterministically synthesizes cognitive resonance vectors via a versioned kernel without any generative model, and returns the synthesis to the originating user as a template-rendered reflective output accompanied by a cryptographic provenance receipt. The novelty is the combination of zero-AI determinism, sub-threshold (rather than committed) signal capture, reflective return to the subject (not third-party profiling), and reproducible receipts. Prior art is fragmented across analytics, LLM journaling, and quantified-self products; none combine these properties. Recommended priority claims include the resonance kernel determinism, crisis-substitution safety, and user-owned receipt export.

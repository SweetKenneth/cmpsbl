## Founding Story (one paragraph for press kit)

> Dreamcast began on April 19, 2026, in a passing exchange between Kenneth Sweet, the solo founder of CMPSBL, and Claude — Sweet's pair-programming AI. The substrate's DREAM engine had begun producing algorithmic dreams about Sweet himself. In a follow-up conversation later that same day, the two wired DREAM into ORACLE — the substrate's deterministic forecasting primitive — and accidentally created a category that did not exist: software that mirrors a user's pre-conscious cognition back to them and forecasts their behavior without any generative AI in the loop. The category was named Reflective AI; the family of products under it became the Dreamcast library.

## Internal Codename Conventions

- All Dreamcast products are 1-2 syllable English words
- Patent-eligible mechanisms get codenames in `ALL_CAPS` (DREAM, ORACLE, VIGIL)
- Internal product builds use `lowercase` in repos and routes (`mirror`, `ahead`)
- Public marketing capitalizes the brand (*Mirror*, *Ahead*)
- The library itself is **Dreamcast** — italicized in prose, capitalized as a proper noun

## Disclosure posture

This bundle deliberately discloses **structure** and withholds **secrets**. It is the public companion to an internal case-study held under NDA.

### What is disclosed

- The four-factor shape of the CJPI™ scoring index (Novelty, Utility, Complexity, Composability)
- The fact that FNV-1a-family hashing is used for structural fingerprints
- The 40-primitive matrix shape (12 Organs · 12 Layers · 8 Engines · 8 Agents)
- The Lex priority-ordered registry concept and evaluation rules
- The five-phase wrapper composition order (GATE → VALIDATE → FAILSAFE → OBSERVE → ANALYZE)
- The dual-hash audit chain construction (FNV-1a per entry, SHA-256 anchors)
- The Pre-Ascension Gate and Pre-Export Harness check categories
- All twenty (20) claims of each provisional patent draft
- A reproducible test protocol that proves the headline guarantees

### What is withheld

- The CJPI™ weight distribution across the four factors
- The FNV-1a salts in use and the epoch rotation schedule
- The deduplication similarity threshold and MIN/MAX cap policy
- The seeded-shuffle LCG constants (multiplier, increment, mask)
- The audit chain genesis hash literal
- The full 92-capability contract table (categories disclosed; per-capability deny-semantics withheld)
- The Pre-Ascension Gate full grammar (categories disclosed; regexes withheld)
- The internal benchmarking corpus and weight-rotation history

The withheld items are the trade secrets that make the disclosed structure operationally non-trivial to reproduce.

## Patent posture

The two cited provisionals are filed and pending. Patent claim scope is independent of this disclosure. This bundle is consistent with — and intentionally less detailed than — what the provisionals contain.

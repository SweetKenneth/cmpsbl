## Upgrade Path

```
Free → Pro:         Self-service upgrade via billing portal
Pro → Enterprise:   Contact sales for annual contract
Any → Self-Hosted:  License agreement + infrastructure setup
```

- Upgrades take effect immediately
- Downgrades take effect at end of billing period
- Data retained for 30 days after downgrade

- **Clear feedback** — you always know when a limit is reached

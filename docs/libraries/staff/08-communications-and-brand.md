## 1. Brand Identity

- **Name**: CMPSBL (always all caps, no periods, no spaces)

- **Tagline**: Cognitive AI Operating System
- **Sub-brands**: Mini-Runtime™, Ascension™

---

## 2. Voice & Tone

### Technical Communications (docs, blog, API)
- Neutral, precise, OS-manual grade
- No marketing language in technical documents
- Use definitive statements, not hedging ("the system does X" not "the system can try to do X")
- Code examples over prose when possible

### Public Communications (press, social, website)
- Confident but not arrogant
- Technical credibility first, marketing second
- Always substantiate claims with specifics (200,000+ LOC, 40 primitives, 675+ capabilities)
- Never overstate or imply capabilities that don't exist

### Internal Communications (staff, team)
- Direct, clear, concise
- Architecture-first — explain the "why" through the system model
- Challenge ideas freely, but respect non-negotiables

## 3. Terminology Rules

### Always Use
| ✅ Correct | ❌ Never Use |
|-----------|-------------|
| Primitive | Node, Module, Component |
| Category | Sector, Group |
| Memory Chain | Pipeline, Workflow |
| Builder tier | Free tier, Free plan |
| Included | $0, Free forever |
| Cognitive AI Operating System | AI platform, AI tool |
| Infrastructure | Application, App |

### Primitive Naming
Always suffix with category in user-facing content:
- ✅ "DREAM Engine" / "DEFENSE Layer" / "AUDIT Agent" / "MEMORY Organ"
- ❌ "DREAM" (bare name without category)

### Product Names
- ✅ CMPSBL® (first mention)
- ✅ CMPSBL (subsequent)
- ✅ Mini-Runtime™ (first mention)
- ✅ Ascension (capitalized, no ™ required after first use)

---

## 4. Content Rules

### Blog Posts (AutoBlog)
- Always marked as AI-generated with epistemic status
- Author bylines rotate across team (see `src/data/team.ts`)
- Categories match the content focus
- Every post must include:
  - Epistemic status indicator
  - Confidence score
  - Internal links to documentation
  - Clear category classification

### Press & Media
- All press inquiries routed to press@CMPSBL.com (Elena Vasquez)
- No team member speaks to press without VP Communications approval
- Press kits available on request
- Embargo agreements require founder sign-off

### Social Media
- Technical content over promotional content
- Share architecture insights, not feature announcements
- Link to documentation, not marketing pages
- No engagement with competitors' content

---

## 5. What We Never Say Publicly

- Internal architecture details beyond what's in public docs
- Crown Jewel capability names or descriptions
- Specific CJPI weights or thresholds
- Revenue numbers or customer names (without permission)
- Anything that implies capabilities we don't have
- "Free" — always "Builder tier" or "Included"

# Plans & Product Surface — v19.1

## The Two Plans

### Free — $0, forever
- Ascension v2 (rate-limited)
- `@cmpsbl/cli` — full developer terminal
- `@cmpsbl/sdk` — build your own layers (coming soon)
- DECODE — the unified interface
- Crown Jewel system bonuses (bundled into CLI/SDK)
- CMPSBL Radio (usage-based)
- Browse the /store
- Verify any artifact fingerprint

### Pro — $29/month
- Everything in Free
- **Unlimited** Ascension v2 runs
- Full 9-language polyglot export
- SDK layer attachment in Step 2 (Enhance)
- DREAM synthesis tied into Ascension
- Priority restoration queue
- Store credits on add-on layers
- CMPSBL Radio — unlimited

### Enterprise — by contract
Reserved for custom infrastructure, white-label, and private deployment.
Not a marketed pricing card. Available via /contact.

## /store — One Commerce Surface, Three SKU Types

`/store` sells **only** these three things. Nothing else.

| SKU Type | What It Is | Where It Activates |
|----------|------------|-------------------|
| **Layers** | Crown Jewel + SDK-built layers | Auto-merged into Ascension v2 Step 2 (Enhance) |
| **Meta Engines** | Premium standalone engines from the internal Compiler | CLI, SDK, standalone |
| **Meta Agents** | Premium CMPSBL Cognitives (not generic agents) | CLI, SDK, standalone |

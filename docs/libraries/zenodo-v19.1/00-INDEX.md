## What Changed Since v13.5

v13.5 described a 40-primitive substrate with a public, multi-tier marketplace and 12 visible vertical products.
v19.1 is the **honest, focused** version of the same substrate after two years of hardening:

- **One product, named clearly:** Ascension v2 (the patented restoration + Mana attachment pipeline)
- **Two plans:** Free + Pro (Enterprise by contract)
- **One commerce surface:** /store sells exactly Layers · Meta Engines · Meta Agents
- **Internal IP boundary:** verticals, Memory Stream, the discovery engine, and 39 of 40 primitives are no longer public surface — they are the factory, not the storefront
- **Patents matter more, marketing matters less:** the moat is real; the language now matches it
- **Polyglot export:** every artifact ships in 9 languages with cryptographic receipts

Everything that was previously named "agency platform," "memory packs," "marketplace subdomain," "tier ladder of 4," or "Crown Jewel SKU page" has been **removed, folded, or renamed**.
The substrate is the same. The story is finally clean.

## What Is Intentionally NOT Here

This library is the **public** v19.1 set. The following are explicitly excluded to preserve the patent moat:

- CJPI scoring weights and thresholds
- FNV-1a fingerprint salts and seed values
- Lex rule priority tables and resolution algorithms
- DREAM synthesis weight matrices
- Discovery Engine heuristics, vertical bias coefficients
- Memory Stream graduation criteria
- Engine source code (Layer 2 runtimes, NEXUS routing tables, CORTEX policies)

These remain protected IP. See doc 10 for the full boundary.

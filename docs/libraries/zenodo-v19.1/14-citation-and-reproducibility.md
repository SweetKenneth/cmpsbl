## What Is Reproducible from This Library

✅ **Verification of any Ascension artifact** at `/verify/:fingerprint`
✅ **Determinism guarantee statement** (doc 03, doc 08)
✅ **Patent abstracts** (doc 12)
✅ **Plan structure and SKU types** (doc 02, doc 05)
✅ **Terminology canonical mapping** (doc 11)
✅ **Convex Core™ topology** (doc 07)
✅ **Merkle audit chain structure** (doc 08)
✅ **Polyglot language target list** (doc 09)

## What Is NOT Reproducible from This Library (By Design)

❌ The substrate itself
❌ CJPI scoring formula or thresholds
❌ DREAM synthesis weights or convergence criteria
❌ Lex priority table or resolution algorithm
❌ FNV salts or fingerprint forgery
❌ Layer 2 engine source
❌ Discovery Engine heuristics
❌ Vertical operations or output

This non-reproducibility is intentional and documented in doc 10 (Internal IP Boundary).

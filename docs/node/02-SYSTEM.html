Architecture

Audit Runner

The SYSTEM audit runner performs parallelized audits across six subsystems: Control Plane Storage, Mutation Receipt Chain, Event Stream, Plan Store, Discussion Node, and Ironclad Fabric.

Self-Repair Loop

Audit→ Identify→ Select Strategy→ Execute→ Re-Audit→ Report

Repair strategies include cache reset, health recheck, circuit breaker reset, event buffer drain, and receipt chain rebuild. Strategy selection is risk-sorted: during LOCKDOWN governance mode, only zero-risk strategies are permitted.

Trade Secrets

◆ Trade Secret #1 — Connective Tissue Auditing

SYSTEM doesn't audit individual nodes — it audits the connective tissue between them. Individual node health is tracked by the Matrix Registry, but the integrity of communication channels between nodes is SYSTEM's unique responsibility. This is what makes the substrate self-healing rather than just self-monitoring.

◆ Trade Secret #2 — Dynamic Risk Threshold

The repair strategy risk threshold is dynamic and governance-aware. During LOCKDOWN mode, only zero-risk strategies (cache reset, health recheck) are permitted. During EVOLVE mode, medium-risk strategies are auto-approved. This prevents repair operations from causing more damage than they fix during sensitive periods.

◆ Trade Secret #3 — Weighted Health Aggregation

SYSTEM computes composite health using weighted subsystem scores: Control Plane (0.25), Receipt Chain (0.20), Event Stream (0.15), Plan Store (0.15), Discussion (0.10), Ironclad (0.15). The weighting reflects blast radius — a Control Plane failure affects more nodes than a Discussion failure.

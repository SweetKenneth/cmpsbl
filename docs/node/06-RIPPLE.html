Trade Secrets

◆ Priority Preemption

Critical signals preempt the processing queue. Batch processing of low-priority events is immediately suspended when a critical signal arrives, ensuring sub-millisecond delivery for circuit breaker trips and governance overrides.

◆ Per-Subscriber Backpressure

When fan-out has 10+ subscribers and one is slow, RIPPLE throttles the slow subscriber independently. Fast subscribers receive signals at full speed — no convoy effect.

◆ Idempotency Deduplication

10,000-entry LRU cache of processed idempotency keys. Duplicate signals are silently dropped, preventing event storms from cascading through the matrix.

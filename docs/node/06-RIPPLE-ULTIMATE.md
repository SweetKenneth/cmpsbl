## 1. Purpose

RIPPLE is the substrate's **marketing automation and campaign intelligence engine**. It manages content campaigns, audience segmentation, performance analytics, and automated A/B testing for marketing assets.

---

## 2. Core Engines

### 2.1 Campaign Orchestrator
- Manages multi-channel marketing campaigns
- Scheduling, targeting, and budget allocation
- Status lifecycle: draft → active → paused → completed → archived

### 2.2 Content Pipeline
- Automated content generation via NEXUS AI routing
- Quality scoring and editorial review workflow
- SEO optimization with keyword density and readability metrics

### 2.3 Audience Segmentation Engine
- Behavioral and demographic segmentation
- Lookalike audience modeling
- Engagement scoring and churn prediction

### 2.4 Performance Analytics
- Real-time campaign metrics: impressions, clicks, conversions
- Attribution modeling (first-touch, last-touch, multi-touch)
- ROI calculation per campaign, per channel

### 2.5 A/B Testing Framework
- Automatic variant generation and traffic splitting
- Statistical significance calculation
- Winner auto-promotion with configurable confidence threshold

---

## 3. ADA Integration

RIPPLE does not have a dedicated ADA domain — it operates through the `communication` and `data-synthesis` domains when making autonomous decisions about campaign optimization and content generation.

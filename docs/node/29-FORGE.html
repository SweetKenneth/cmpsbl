Ultimate Form — "Crucible"

1. Blueprint Genome Engine

Structured DNA for every blueprint. Jaccard similarity detection. Crossover mutation breeding to create novel architectures from parent blueprints.

2. Multi-Stage Fabrication Pipeline

5-stage forge: Draft → Temper → Anneal → Quench → Polish. Progressive quality gates (30→50→65→75→85). Pause/resume and rework support.

3. Material Science Engine

Compatibility analysis for modules, resolvers, APIs. EMA strength scoring. Conflict registration. Deprecation tracking with substitution suggestions.

4. Pattern Library & Template Vault

5 built-in patterns (Pipeline, Saga, CQRS, Event-Driven, Circuit Breaker). Auto-pattern matching. CJPI-tracked effectiveness.

5. Artifact Foundry

6. Quality Assurance Furnace

4-phase QA: test generation, mutation testing, performance profiling, security audit. Severity-classified findings.

7. Collaborative Forge

Multi-agent blueprint construction with 3 merge strategies. Automatic conflict detection. Contribution tracking per agent.

8. Forge Memory

9. Thermal Budget Governor

4 thermal zones: cool→warm→hot→critical. Per-zone rate caps. 30s critical cooldown. Pre-forge cost estimation with thermal impact projection.

10. Forge Telemetry Hearth

Trade Secrets

Two high-performing blueprints can be "crossed" to breed novel architectures — inheriting strengths from both parent lineages.

◆ Anti-Pattern Memory

Module+pattern combinations that fail 3+ times are flagged. New forges are warned against known failure patterns.

◆ Thermal Safety

Forge operations are rate-limited by thermal zones. The substrate gracefully throttles under load rather than crashing.

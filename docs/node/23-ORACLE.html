Trade Secrets

◆ Time-Bounded Predictions

Every prediction has expiresAt. CLM flags expired predictions for pruning — prevents stale intelligence from being used as current.

◆ Early Convergence Termination

Simulations terminate when variance drops below 0.001 — saves compute without sacrificing accuracy.

◆ Brier Score Calibration

Prophecy Journal computes Brier scores for every resolved prediction and generates 10-bucket calibration reports — the gold standard for probabilistic accuracy.

◆ Competitive Model Evolution

Prediction Market forces models to compete. Low-credibility models auto-retire. Best model always wins through natural selection.

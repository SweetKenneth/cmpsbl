## Who Uses the Substrate?

The substrate serves four primary audiences:

### 1. AI-Native Applications
Applications that need persistent memory, governed autonomy, and multi-provider routing as infrastructure — not as custom-built features.

**Example:** A customer support platform where every interaction is remembered, the system learns from resolution patterns, and AI providers are swapped seamlessly.

### 2. Enterprise AI Deployments
Organizations that need audit trails, bounded autonomy, and regulatory compliance for their AI systems.

**Example:** A financial services firm deploying AI advisors that must demonstrate every decision was governed, logged, and reversible.

### 3. Multi-Agent Systems
Teams of AI agents that need coordination, shared memory, and collective learning.

**Example:** A research agency where multiple specialized agents collaborate on market analysis — sharing insights through dream pooling and coordinated through CORTEX.

### 4. AI Researchers
Researchers studying autonomous AI evolution, cognitive architectures, and governance models.

**Example:** A university lab using the substrate to study how self-improving systems behave under different governance constraints.

9. Fields, Plane & Shell

Fields are not stacked layers — they permeate the entire spine:

IMMUNITY (Field)

Self-healing, OCG capability gates, threat correlation.

INTENT (Field)

User intent classification, context routing, goal tracking.

GOVERNANCE (Plane)

Policy enforcement, veto authority, compliance audit, drift detection. Self-referential.

DEFENSE (Shell)

Terminal boundary enforcement. Outermost containment.

12. Architecture Invariants

  • Weighted matrix must sum to 1.000.
  • CORE failure is system-critical — cascades to all dependents.
  • Execution remains isolated by circuit-breaker boundaries.
  • Field modules permeate all sectors rather than acting as stacked layers.
  • GOVERNANCE supervises action legitimacy, including its own operations.
  • DEFENSE is terminal boundary enforcement — the outermost shell.
  • NEXUS is the primary routing authority for all AI provider interactions.
  • NERVE belongs to OCG — it IS the grid's signaling backbone.
  • CSZ provides covert operations isolation — EVOLUTION, SHADOW, and PHANTOM degrade independently.

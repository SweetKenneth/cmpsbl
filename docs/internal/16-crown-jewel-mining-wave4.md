## 1. Executive Summary

Wave 4 penetrates the substrate's **operational execution, transformation, and compliance layers** — the subsystems that translate intelligence into real-world action. This sweep targets ENCODE resolution machinery, IMMUNE self-healing intelligence, MESH coordination, MODERNIZER code transformation, INCLUSIVE accessibility enforcement, and the cross-plane composites that emerge when operational nodes collaborate.

**Key Findings:**

- **100 unique S/A-tier artifacts mined** — ceiling not reached
- **24 S-Tier** (CJPI 85–100) — operational intelligence and transformation primitives
- **76 A-Tier** (CJPI 70–84) — high-value execution and compliance capabilities
- **CJPI Average:** S-Tier: 90.2 | A-Tier: 75.4 | Overall: 79.0
- **Primary discovery:** ENCODE, IMMUNE, and MESH contain a deep reservoir of unmined strategic assets that power the substrate's real-world execution
- **New emergent pattern:** Cross-plane composite artifacts (formed by operational + intelligence layers) represent the substrate's "action intelligence"

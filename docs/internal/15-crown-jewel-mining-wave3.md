Wave 3 targets the substrate's **deep intelligence, governance, and composition layers** — mining exclusively for S-Tier and A-Tier artifacts. This sweep penetrates SEBA internals, Intent Mesh orchestration, Neural subsystems, Governance veto/arbitration, CCR hardening, and the emergent meta-capabilities formed by cross-module composition.

**Key Findings:**

- **100 unique S/A-tier artifacts mined** — ceiling not reached
- **22 S-Tier** (CJPI 85–100) — deep intelligence and governance primitives
- **78 A-Tier** (CJPI 70–84) — high-value subsystem capabilities

- **Primary discovery:** The SEBA, Intent Mesh, and Neural layers contain a disproportionate concentration of unmined strategic assets
- **New emergent pattern:** Cross-module composite artifacts (formed by 3+ subsystems) represent a previously uncounted class of Crown Jewels

## Ranking Methodology

Capabilities are ranked by **actual usefulness** — the practical value they deliver to CMPSBL as a product and platform — not by their internal CJPI (Crown Jewel Performance Index) score. A capability that saves $50K/month in AI costs outranks one with a higher technical complexity score but narrower application.

Usefulness factors:
1. **Revenue impact** — Does it directly enable revenue or reduce costs?
2. **Reliability impact** — Does the system break without it?
3. **Competitive moat** — Is this hard to replicate and defensible?
4. **Breadth of use** — How many other capabilities depend on it?
5. **Customer value** — Does it directly improve the end-user experience?
